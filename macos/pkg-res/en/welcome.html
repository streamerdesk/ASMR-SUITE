Streamerdesk ASMR Suite

Six audio effects for OBS Studio, lovingly made for ASMR creators: Ping-Pong Echo, Reverb, 8D / Orbit, Ear-to-Ear Whisper, Proximity and Binaural Spatializer.

At the Installation Type step, click Customize to choose which effects to install (all are selected).

They go into your OBS user folder (~/Library/Application Support/obs-studio/plugins). Your presets are kept. Please quit OBS Studio before continuing.

Requires OBS Studio 28 or later and macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel).
